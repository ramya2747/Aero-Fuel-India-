1. Permitted Use

AERO // FUEL INDIA is provided as an operational logging and tracking tool for personal, transport, fleet, and commercial fuel recording across Indian states and territories. You may use this software free of charge for recording, analyzing, and backing up your fuel metrics.

2. User Accounts and Data Responsibility

AERO // FUEL INDIA supports both guest operation and authenticated cloud accounts via Supabase:

  • Account Confidentiality: You are responsible for safeguarding your login credentials. We will not be liable for unauthorized access resulting from compromised passwords.
  • Guest Mode Data: When operating without an account, records exist solely in your local browser storage. You are responsible for exporting backups to avoid data loss from browser cache clearing.
  • Cloud Account Data: When signed in, records are stored in Supabase Postgres under your authenticated account ID. You may export or delete your records at any time.

3. Calculations and Fuel Rates Notice

Total amounts are calculated based on user-entered values using the formula: Litres consumed multiplied by price per litre. Figures are rounded to two decimal places. Users should verify numbers against actual retail fuel dispenser receipts for tax, regulatory, or corporate accounting purposes.
