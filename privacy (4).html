1. Overview and Storage Architecture

AERO // FUEL INDIA provides two storage modes depending on whether you choose to use an account:

  • Guest Mode (Unauthenticated): If you do not sign in, all fuel log records, vehicle categories, locations, and rates remain strictly in your browser local storage on your device.
  • Cloud Account Mode (Authenticated): If you sign up and sign in, your fuel records are transmitted over encrypted network connections (HTTPS) to Supabase cloud servers and stored in a private Postgres database.

2. Cloud Storage and Row Level Security

When you use an account, your data is managed through Supabase:

  • Records are stored in a dedicated Postgres database table (public.fuel_records).
  • Access is enforced through database-level Row Level Security (RLS). Every query checks that the record owner matches your authenticated account identifier. No other user can read or modify your records.
  • Unauthenticated visitors are explicitly blocked by database grants from querying user records.

3. Local Storage and Data Synchronization

If you start in guest mode and later create an account:

  • The application checks your browser storage key (aero_fuel_records_v1) and asks for your confirmation before syncing any records to the cloud.
  • Original local records remain preserved on your computer and are marked as synced. They are never automatically erased.
  • Sync operations use unique keys to ensure that retrying an upload never creates duplicate entries.

4. Backups and File Exports

Whether signed in or in guest mode, you retain full ownership of your records. You can export complete CSV spreadsheets or JSON backups at any time directly through the application. File parsing for CSV imports and JSON restorations occurs locally in your browser.

5. External Tracking and Cookies

This application does not load third-party marketing cookies, Google Analytics, social media widgets, or advertising trackers. Network communication is limited strictly to authenticating and syncing your fuel log entries with Supabase.
